Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This privacy policy covers every app that Filip Nordqvist ("we", "us") publishes on Google Play under the developer name Filip Nordqvist (the "apps"). It explains what information the apps handle, who receives it, and the choices you have.

In short

  • We don't run any servers for our apps, and none of our apps sends information to us.
  • Apps without ads don't collect or share any information.
  • Ad-supported apps show ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information from your device to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. On Google Play, these apps are marked "Contains ads".
  • Everything else you do in an app stays on your device.

The Data safety section on each app's Google Play page also shows what that app collects and shares.

Apps without ads

Apps without ads don't collect or share any personal information. They don't send anything to us or to anyone else. What you enter or create in them stays on your device, as described under In all our apps.

Ad-supported apps

Ad-supported apps are free because they show ads served by Google AdMob. Google's ads software in these apps may collect the following and send it to Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and the app set ID
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as your city or region) may be estimated
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown and tapped
  • diagnostic information about how the ads perform, such as load times and errors

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and abuse and, where you allow it, personalize ads. Google is responsible for the information it collects and handles it under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We don't receive this information. AdMob only gives us reports with totals, such as how many ads were shown, which don't identify you.

Consent. Where the law requires consent, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for it through Google's consent form before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice later in the app's settings. Without consent you may still see ads that aren't personalized. Google still uses some information to show them, to limit how often they repeat and to prevent fraud.

Your ad choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and manage ad personalization in Google's My Ad Center.

Advertisers. Ads are chosen by Google, not by us. If you tap an ad, the advertiser's site or app and its own privacy policy apply.

In all our apps

  • Stored on your device. What you enter or create, such as settings, progress, scores and lists, is kept in the app's own storage on your device. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't sent to us. It's deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Backups. If you've turned on backup on your device, the system may include this data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account and handled under Google's privacy policy. We can't see your backups.
  • Device features and permissions. Some apps use features of your device, such as vibration, sound, text-to-speech, printing or the file picker. These run on your device, and nothing passed to them is sent to us. If an app asks for a permission, it's only for a feature of that app. You can refuse it, or remove it later in your device settings.
  • Purchases. If an app offers a purchase, such as removing ads, Google Play handles the payment. Google Play gives us a record of the order (such as an order number, the product and the date) so the purchase can be unlocked. We never see your payment details.
  • Crash reports and statistics. Google Play gives every developer statistics such as install numbers, and crash and performance reports from devices whose owners have agreed to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These reports don't identify you. We use them to find and fix problems.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix or improve the apps.

How information is used

  • to make each app's features work on your device;
  • to show and measure ads in ad-supported apps, and to prevent ad fraud;
  • to unlock what you bought;
  • to find and fix crashes and improve the apps;
  • to answer your messages;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We don't use information for any other purpose, and we don't build profiles of you.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent: personalized ads, and anything else an app asks your permission for. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: showing ads that aren't personalized so free apps can stay free, preventing ad fraud, fixing crashes and answering your messages.
  • Contract: giving you what you bought.
  • Legal obligation: keeping or disclosing information when the law requires it.

Who else receives information

Our apps don't send information to us. The only other party that receives information from them is Google: through AdMob in ad-supported apps, and through Google Play and device backups as described above. Google may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using the safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy.

We disclose information we hold, such as emails you've sent us, only when the law requires it, or when it's needed to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us.

How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Your emails to us: as long as needed to deal with your message, then deleted. We delete them sooner if you ask.
  • Information collected by Google: according to Google's retention policy.

Your choices and rights

  • Uninstall an app, or clear its data, to remove everything it stored on your device.
  • Turn off personalized ads through the app's settings, your device settings or My Ad Center.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at filipnordquist@protonmail.com. We answer within the time the law requires.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California), you may also have the right to object to or restrict how your information is used, to receive a copy of it, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Since the apps don't send information to us, the only information we may hold about you is what you've emailed us. For ad data, Google's own tools are the way to see or delete it.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

What our apps store stays in each app's own storage on your device, which the system keeps separate from other apps. Because the apps don't send information to us, there's no copy on our side that could be lost or leaked. Google's ads and payment services send information over encrypted connections. No way of storing or sending information is completely secure, but these measures keep the risk low.

This website

This page uses no cookies, ads or analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when an app adds ads or a new feature. We'll post the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what an app collects, we'll update that app's Data safety section at the same time.
